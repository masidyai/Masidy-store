# Masidy Brand Update

The storefront now uses the supplied Masidy wordmark and M symbol with the
brand's black, white, and vermilion palette.

## Brand assets

- `public/masidy-wordmark-light.png` is used on light surfaces.
- `public/masidy-wordmark-dark.png` is used on dark surfaces.
- `public/masidy-icon-light.png` and `public/masidy-icon-dark.png` are the
  app and browser icons for light and dark browser themes.
- `public/masidy-icon-maskable.png` is the safe-area app icon.
- `public/logo.svg` and the `public/icon*.svg` files provide scalable variants.

The palette is near-black `#1C1C1C`, white `#FFFFFF`, and vermilion `#FC2F02`.
Shared CSS tokens keep interactive accents and surfaces consistent throughout
the site. Product names, catalog data, and prices have not been changed.
